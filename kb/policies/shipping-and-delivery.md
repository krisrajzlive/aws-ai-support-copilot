# Shipping and delivery

Acme Home Goods is a fictional company; these policies are invented for testing.

## Delivery times
Standard delivery within the country takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2
business days. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days.

## Where we ship
We ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. We do not ship to
PO boxes outside the United States.

## Tracking and late parcels
Every order has a tracking number in the dispatch email. If a parcel has not arrived 10 business days
after dispatch, or tracking has not updated for 5 business days, support will open a trace with the
carrier and send a replacement or refund once the trace confirms the parcel is lost.

## Changing an address
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is dispatched. After dispatch the carrier
must be contacted, and support can open that request.
