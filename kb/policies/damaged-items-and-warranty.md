# Damaged items and warranty

Acme Home Goods is a fictional company; these policies are invented for testing.

## Items that arrive damaged
Report damage within 14 days of delivery. Send a photo of the item and of the packaging. We then
choose a replacement or a full refund, and we cover return shipping. Customers do not need to
troubleshoot an item that arrived visibly broken.

## Warranty
Electrical products carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing faults. The warranty does not
cover accidental damage, water damage or normal wear. For a fault, support asks for the order number
and a short description, and may ask for a photo or video of the problem.

## Safety
If a product overheats, smokes or sparks, the customer should stop using it immediately. Support
treats these reports as high priority.
