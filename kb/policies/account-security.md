# Account security

Acme Home Goods is a fictional company; these policies are invented for testing.

## Passwords and resets
Password reset links expire after 30 minutes. Support never asks for a password, a full card number
or a verification code, by any channel.

## Suspicious activity
If a customer received a password reset they did not request, or suspects someone else used their
account, support asks them to reset the password from the official sign-in page and enable
two-step verification. The case is flagged urgent for the security team.

## Locked accounts
An account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts and unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or sooner
once identity is verified by the security team.
