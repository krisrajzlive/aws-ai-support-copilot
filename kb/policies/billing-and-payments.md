# Billing and payments

Acme Home Goods is a fictional company; these policies are invented for testing.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
A duplicate charge is reversed automatically within 3 to 5 business days. If it is still on the
statement after 5 business days, support will refund it manually once the customer provides the order
number.

## Invoices and receipts
Invoices and receipts are available in the customer's account under Orders, and a copy is emailed at
purchase. Support can resend an invoice on request.

## Subscriptions
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the paid period. A charge
made after cancellation is refunded in full.
