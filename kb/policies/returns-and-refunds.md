# Returns and refunds

Acme Home Goods is a fictional company used to ground this project's replies. These policies are
invented for testing and do not describe any real business.

## Return window
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused
and in their original packaging. Gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. After the returned item is received and inspected,
the refund is issued within 3 business days and appears on the customer's statement within 5 to 7
business days.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free when the item is faulty, damaged or the wrong item was sent. For any other
reason the customer pays return postage, which is deducted from the refund.
